Benefits:
🌍 Internet ke kahin se bhi access ho sakta hai — best for servers, websites, remote access.
📌 Device ko globally unique identify karta hai.
🔗 Direct peer-to-peer communication possible hota hai.
❌ Drawbacks:
📉 Limited availability (IPv4 exhaustion issue).
🛡️ Hacking & DDoS attacks ka zyada risk.
💰 Usually zyada expensive hota hai (ISP assign karta hai).

🔹 Private IP Address
✅ Benefits:
🔒 Internal network ke liye zyada secure (internet pe directly expose nahi hota).
🔄 Same IP ranges ko alag-alag networks me reuse kar sakte ho.
♻️ Public IPs ko save karta hai NAT (Network Address Translation) use karke.
❌ Drawbacks:
🚫 Public internet pe directly route nahi hota (direct access possible nahi).
🌐 Internet access ke liye NAT ki requirement hoti hai.
⚙️ Remote access ya hosting setup me thoda complex ho sakta hai.

Accessible from anywhere on the internet — ideal for servers, websites, remote access.
        Uniquely identifies a device on the global internet
        Supports direct peer-to-peer communication.

    ❌ Drawbacks:
        Limited availability (IPv4 exhaustion).
        More vulnerable to hacking and DDoS attacks.
        Often more expensive (assigned by ISPs).

    Private IP Address

    ✅ Benefits:
        More secure for internal networks (not directly exposed to the internet).
        Allows reuse of the same IP ranges in different networks.
        Conserves public IP space (via NAT — Network Address Translation).

    ❌ Drawbacks:
        Not routable on the public internet (can’t be accessed directly from outside).
        Requires NAT to communicate externally.
        May complicate configurations for remote access or hosting
